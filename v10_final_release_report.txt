HUMAN AUDIT CRITERIA FOR EACH PASSAGE
1. The passage has one coherent topic/scene and a clear beginning-development-end flow.
2. Each sentence follows naturally from the previous sentence; no vocabulary-dump sentence may be inserted just because a word is available.
3. Pronouns/references are unambiguous.
4. English is idiomatic enough for a junior-high teaching text; grammatically possible but unnatural English fails.
5. The grammar does not exceed the selected textbook/grade/section prerequisite gate.
6. Vocabulary does not exceed the cumulative textbook ceiling. Unlearned words are avoided; annotations are exceptional, not a workaround.
7. Full Japanese translation is natural and matches the English exactly.
8. Slash-reading chunks follow meaning units and support left-to-right comprehension; slash Japanese is not treated as the natural full translation.
9. Questions test actual comprehension of the passage. Answers/evidence/explanations must match the revised text exactly.
10. A and B question sets must remain valid after any rewrite.
11. The result should resemble the quality and instructional purpose of the supplied long-reading examples, not a list of short unrelated example sentences.
