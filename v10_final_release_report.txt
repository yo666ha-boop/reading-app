HUMAN AUDIT CRITERIA FOR EACH PASSAGE
1. The passage has one coherent topic/scene and a clear beginning-development-end flow.
2. Each sentence follows naturally from the previous sentence; no vocabulary-dump sentence may be inserted just because a word is available.
3. When appropriate, include a small change/problem/decision and a natural ending, following the supplied curated long-reading examples rather than producing a flat list of facts.
4. Pronouns/references are unambiguous.
5. English is idiomatic enough for a junior-high teaching text; grammatically possible but unnatural English fails.
6. The grammar does not exceed the selected textbook/grade/section prerequisite gate.
7. Vocabulary does not exceed the cumulative textbook ceiling. Unlearned words are avoided; annotations are exceptional, not a workaround. Available vocabulary is a ceiling, not a checklist to consume.
8. Full Japanese translation is natural and matches the English exactly.
9. Slash-reading chunks follow meaning units and support left-to-right comprehension; slash Japanese is not treated as the natural full translation.
10. Questions test actual comprehension of the passage, including sequence/change/reason/summary when the text supports them. Answers/evidence/explanations must match the revised text exactly.
11. A and B question sets must remain valid after any rewrite.
12. The result should resemble the quality and instructional purpose of the supplied long-reading examples, not a list of short unrelated example sentences.
