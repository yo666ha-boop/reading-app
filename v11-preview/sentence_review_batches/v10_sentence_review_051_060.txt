PASSAGE 052 | 中1 New Horizon Unit 5-1 | REWRITE_MAJOR
Problems: “This is my brother / his blog” begins in one narrator voice, then the text suddenly switches to first-person “I like nature / I work as a local guide / I write ... / I enjoy ...” without telling the reader that the blog writer is now speaking. The speaker identity is broken. Rebuild either as the brother’s blog post in first person or as the sibling describing the brother consistently in third person.

The dolphin picture does not develop into an actual experience, and “Interesting!” is unsupported. Rebuild as a short blog entry or description about one dolphin/swimming experience instead of interrogating unrelated habits.

Keep the cafe topic but make the conversation setup and ending natural.

PASSAGE 060 | 中1 New Horizon Unit 7-3 | REWRITE_MAJOR
