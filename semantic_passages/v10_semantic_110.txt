The Shoes-at-the-Door RuleV10-NH-G2-U4-4-001 / ニューホライズン / 中2 / Unit 4-4During my homestay, I stayed with an elderly couple. On the first evening, I entered the house with my shoes on. The couple stopped me at the door. At first, I couldn’t understand the rule. I felt nervous because I thought I had done something wrong. The couple explained that I should take off my shoes before I enter the room. I listened to the couple carefully. Then I took off my shoes at the door. The couple smiled, and I felt better.
