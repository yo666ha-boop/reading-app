An Article That Makes Our Class Take ActionV10-NH-G3-U3-2-001 / ニューホライズン / 中3 / Unit 3-2I read an article about an endangered animal near our town. The article says that the animal is losing places to find food. I had heard of the animal before, but I did not know about this problem. I show the article to my friend. I want my friend to read the part about the animal’s habitat. My friend asks me to explain why the problem is getting worse. I tell her to look at the map in the article. The map shows where the habitat has become smaller. We ask our teacher to let us share the article with the class. Then we make a short list of actions that can help protect the habitat.
