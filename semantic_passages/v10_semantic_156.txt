Why Sea Otters Needed ProtectionV10-NH-G3-U3-4-001 / ニューホライズン / 中3 / Unit 3-4Sea otters once lived in large numbers along part of the coast. Heavy hunting caused the population to fall sharply. Later, oil pollution in the sea created another danger. Oil can damage an otter’s fur and make survival difficult. Researchers studied the remaining population and its role in the ecosystem. Their research showed that sea otters were important to coastal life. The animal was given stronger protection in many areas. Citizens and researchers also watched the population over time.
