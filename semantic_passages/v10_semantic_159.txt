Reopening the Bridge to the ShelterV10-NH-G3-U4-3-001 / ニューホライズン / 中3 / Unit 4-3After a serious disaster, one bridge to the community shelter was damaged. People had to use a longer route while the bridge was closed. The town repaired the bridge so residents could reach the shelter safely again. On the day it reopened, students helped guide elderly people along the route. One student comforted a friend who still felt afraid after the disaster. The community also held a quiet moment to remember people who had died. The event did not erase the loss or fear. It showed that safe infrastructure and human support are both important after a disaster. Residents can now use the bridge as the main route to the shelter.
