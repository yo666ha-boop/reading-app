Why the Cape Floral Region Is SpecialV10-NH-G2-U7-2-001 / ニューホライズン / 中2 / Unit 7-2The Cape Floral Region is a World Heritage site. It is a natural heritage site. The region has a great diversity of plant life. Many different plants grow in a small area. Its floral world is fantastic. This diversity gives the region important natural value. At a conference, people can discuss how to protect this heritage. The region is different from a cultural heritage site. It is also different from a mixed site, which has both natural and cultural value. In general, World Heritage sites can have different kinds of value.
