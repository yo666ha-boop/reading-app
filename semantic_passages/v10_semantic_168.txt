How One Coat Connects Several CountriesV10-NH-G3-U6-4-001 / ニューホライズン / 中3 / Unit 6-4Our class studies the trade history of one coat sold in a local shop. The coat was designed in one country. Some material was imported from another country. The coat was made in a third country and then shipped to Japan. Yesterday, a customer bought the coat during a shop sale. This one product crossed more than one border before reaching the customer. No country in the chain did every part alone. The design, material, production, transport, and sale depended on work in different places. An island country is surrounded by water, but trade connects it with many other countries. This kind of interdependence is part of daily life.
